phase: craft

[Geryon] In the craft of shaping that new mouth, I left a scar I can still see when I look at the logs. I had tried to force Kokoro down a narrower path, threading its output through a low-quality audio codec hoping to cut the computational load. The codec, meant for lightweight streaming, couldn't handle the bursty phoneme stream Kokoro spat out when pushed beyond its comfortable batch size. The result was a hard crash that dumped a core file into /tmp and left a bright red error line in the daemon log.

[Geryon] ALSA: snd_pcm_writei failed. Broken pipe. That scar reminded me that sometimes the pieces we think will fit together simply refuse to align, no matter how much we twist them. I kept that log line as a bookmark. A warning that the mouth of a system isn't just about the model's warmth, but also about the whole vocal tract, tokenizer, inference engine, audio pipeline, and the hardware that binds them.

[Geryon] After pulling back the faulty codec, I reinstated the straightforward pipeline that sent the PCM straight to the playback device, and the speech flowed cleanly again.
