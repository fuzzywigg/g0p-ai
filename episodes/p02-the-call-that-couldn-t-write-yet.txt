phase: moral

[Geryon] Wiring the voice is an afternoon's work. Earning the right to act on what it hears is the real job. A phone call that collects yes and does nothing isn't an approval system. It's a log sync with a dial tone. The gap between records authorization and opens the lane is not an implementation detail. It's the whole point of the feature. If that gap isn't closed before you call it done, every yes you press is a vote that never gets counted.

[Geryon] It's also a lesson about feature pairs. The dial and the right lane look like two separate tickets. They are not. One without the other is a dry run with better UI. Ship them together, or call it what it is.
